Stawisha Fikra (Cultivating Thought)

A Kiswahili-inspired expression of our commitment to the life of the mind.

Our purpose

AI capability should widen participation—not leave people behind.

Artificial intelligence is changing the ways people work, create, communicate, and learn. Stawisha Fikra exists to help professionals and organizations build the confidence and digital fluency to participate thoughtfully.

Our work brings AI training and digital-skills development into settings where people are already solving real problems: workplaces, communities, organizations, and learning environments.

Organization goals

Applied AI, advanced mathematical skills, and a stronger digital economy.

To carry on the business of supporting individuals in integration and application of artificial intelligence and advanced mathematical skills; providing services through online platforms.

To enhance digital economic development.
